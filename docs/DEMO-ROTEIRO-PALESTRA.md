# VisualContext — demo film and talk notes (Hamburg, 05/11/2026)

[🇬🇧 English](#en--english) | [🇧🇷 Português](#pt--português)

## EN — English

**What it is.** A 3-minute concept film that plays on its own in a browser: `web/demo-film.html` (published as an artifact; captions in EN, DE, PT). It is the simulator, not footage from the glasses: the title says so. Keyboard: space play/pause · ← → scenes · L language · H hide the bar · F full screen.

**How to record the screencast.** Open the film in full screen, press H to hide the bar, press space, record the screen (OBS, QuickTime, or Windows+G) for 3 min. Optional: narrate over it with the notes below.

**Notes for the presenter (one line per scene, ~15 s each):**
1. **Title.** VisualContext: a memory layer for smart glasses, built on Rokid Glasses in São Paulo.
2. **Standby.** Nothing on the lens until you need it. A dot says the memory is on.
3. **Meeting someone.** The face is recognised on the device, nothing leaves the phone. Name, how you know each other, last time. The recording announces itself with a countdown you can cancel.
4. **Several people.** A name beside each head; the card follows whoever you look at.
5. **Unknown face.** A small mark only. Nothing stored unless you say so.
6. **The key.** Teach it once with the key in hand. "Where is my key?" shows the last photo where it was seen.
7. **The motorbike.** The parking moment is detected; two photos (bike, surroundings). "Did the key come with you?" if it stayed on the bike.
8. **Places.** Up to two cards pinned to the buildings; dashed when hidden behind another one.
9. **Recall.** Tap or AI button, ask in plain words. Open questions go to a live voice assistant that sees what you see (Gemini Live).
10. **Manager (phone).** Ask anything, every answer has its source. "My day". Actions only on tap.
11. **Modules.** General, Business, Low vision (spoken cards), Deaf (live captions). Accessibility is the launch hook.
12. **Creepy or not creepy.** On-device faces, announced recording, lock when the glasses come off, forget everything.

**Status, honest.** Working today: face recognition on the phone/glasses (ArcFace), Gemini Live in the glasses, the Manager with 95 people. Still prototype: places (web prototype), key/motorbike (spec), modules (spec). Nothing in the film is faked beyond being drawn instead of filmed.

## PT — Português

**O que é.** Um filme-conceito de 3 minutos que roda sozinho no navegador: `web/demo-film.html` (publicado como artefato; legendas em EN, DE, PT). É o simulador, não imagem dos óculos: o título diz isso. Teclado: espaço play/pause · ← → cenas · L idioma · H esconde a barra · F tela cheia.

**Como gravar o screencast.** Abra o filme em tela cheia, aperte H para esconder a barra, aperte espaço e grave a tela (OBS, QuickTime ou Windows+G) por 3 min. Opcional: narrar por cima com as notas acima.

**Notas para quem apresenta (uma linha por cena):** as mesmas 12 linhas acima, em inglês, porque a plateia é em Hamburgo.

**Status, sem enfeite.** Funciona hoje: reconhecimento de rostos no celular/óculos (ArcFace), Gemini Live nos óculos, o Manager com 95 pessoas. Ainda protótipo: Lugares (protótipo web), chave/moto (spec), módulos (spec). Nada no filme é falso além de ser desenhado em vez de filmado.
